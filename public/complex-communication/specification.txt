Summary:


     An Applied Session to bring together the concepts discussed during the semester
     regarding effective communication and communication planning. You will undertake
     a two hour intensive workshop simulating a critical incident in an organisation and
     how to plan the communications around this incident.

     The FIT5122 teams will report their findings to their manager (the tutor).

     The Scenario

        ●​ A 2-hour intensive workshop simulating a critical IT incident.
        ●​ Participants work in teams of 4-6 people, with each team member must
           participate and contribute to the work.
        ●​ A scenario will be given to you in your applied session.
        ●​ Printed templates will be given to you by your tutor.

Objectives:


     By the end of this workshop, teams will demonstrate their knowledge of the
     communications theories (Shannon/Weaver and Laswell models and Schramm’s
     insights) and communication design (Empathy Forecast) and their ability to:



        1.​ Define the issue(s) in the scenario
        2.​ Identity and understand the stakeholders impacted by the issue(s)
               a.​ Use Empathy Forecasting to understand the issues/concerns of each
                   stakeholder
               b.​ Identify the questions that will need to be answered by the
                   communication plan for each stakeholder.
        3.​ Create Stakeholder Communication Approach
               a.​ Create comprehensive stakeholder communications that addresses
                   the concerns of all stakeholders
               b.​ What “messages” should be communicated, and to which stakeholders
               c.​ Identify and explain how different channel communication strategies
                   can be used to communicate with each stakeholder
               d.​ Identify the feedback collection mechanisms
        4.​ Consider Ethical Issues around Communications

Instructions:

     You will develop a comprehensive communication plan for approval from your
     manager.

     Solve communication challenges faced by a specific organization or community
     based on the given scenario. You will define the stakeholders and use empathy
     forecasting to understand the questions that have to be answered for each
     stakeholder.

     You will then develop a communication approach to address this challenge, drawing
     upon communication theories and best practices learned throughout the semester
     for approval from your manager.




     Workshop Flow:

        1.​ Workshop Briefing and Q&A (10 minutes)
        2.​ Review Scenario, identify the stakeholders and their communications
            requirements (30 mins)
        3.​ Complete the template - Empathy Forecast and Communication plan (40min)
        4.​ At the end, before handing in your work to your tutor scan and submit your
            documents to Moodle
        5.​ Tutors will collect filled out templates at the end of the applied workshop



     Steps:

        ●​ Printed template will be handed out to the teams in the tutorial
        ●​ Empathy forecast template (one for each stakeholder)


General expectations:

        ●​ Team collaboration, every member of the team actively participates
        ●​ Well-written with clear handwriting
        ●​ Good quality submitted file to Moodle
